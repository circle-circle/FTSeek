#### 1. 配置持久化


#### 2. 服务监控

```bash
# 添加自动重启
# /etc/systemd/system/ftseek.service
[Unit]
Description=FTSeek Trading System
After=network.target

[Service]
Type=simple
ExecStart=/opt/ftseek/FTSeek/start.sh
Restart=always
RestartSec=10

[Install]
WantedBy=multi-user.target
```
